Built on World of Dungeons by John Harper, licensed under CC BY 3.0; its rules are paraphrased and adapted here. Attributes are rolled 2d6 in order: 6 or less is +0, 7–9 is +1, 10–11 is +2, 12 is +3. The standard array (+2, +1, +1, 0, 0, −1, not in the official rules) is an alternative, shuffled or assigned by hand; CON then sets the hit dice, at least one. True names are the game's own; other names, spirits, quirks and motivations are original tables. The same seed, class and quirk setting always roll the same character.
